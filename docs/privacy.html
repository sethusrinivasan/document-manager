Privacy Policy — Paperstow

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Paperstow ("the App") is a local vault for travel papers on Android. Passports, visas, tickets, and similar files stay on your device. The App has no backend server and does not create an account.

Data collection

Optional, on-device only: usage telemetry (opt-in) stores anonymous feature-use counts locally. No document content, file names, or personal identifiers. Nothing is sent unless you choose to share a report.

The App does not collect document content, personal identification information, location or GPS history, contacts, or phone numbers. Photos are imported only when you choose them.

Data storage

Documents, metadata, and settings are stored locally. Files are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using Android KeyStore. The App does not transmit documents unless you use Android's share sheet or export a password-protected archive to a folder you choose.

Permissions

Denying camera permission does not break file or folder import, viewing, search, or share.

Third-party libraries

ML Kit Text Recognition runs on your device. Google's ML Kit may download its recognition model on first use. Document images are not uploaded to a Paperstow server.

Data sharing

We do not sell or share your data. If you use Share or Archive, you choose the destination.

Children's privacy

This App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

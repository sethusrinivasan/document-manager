Privacy Policy — Paperstow

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Paperstow ("the App") is a local vault for travel papers on Android. Package name: com.app.paperstow. Passports, visas, tickets, notes, and similar files stay on your device. The App has no backend server and does not create an account.

Data collection

Optional, on-device only:

  • Usage telemetry (opt-in, off by default) stores anonymous feature-use counts locally. No document content, file names, or personal identifiers. Nothing is sent unless you choose to share a report.
  • My Trail (opt-in): if you start it, unique places from about the last 24 hours and battery level at each save stay on this device. Paperstow does not upload those points. You may share a Maps link yourself.

The App does not collect document content unless you share or export it, does not create an account, and does not read contacts. Photos are imported only when you choose them. Location is used only if you start My Trail.

Data storage

Documents, metadata, tags, settings, and My Trail points are stored locally. Document files are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using Android KeyStore. The App does not transmit documents unless you use Android's share sheet or export a ZIP archive to a folder you choose. A backup password is optional; an unprotected ZIP contains readable document bytes.

Third-party libraries

ML Kit Text Recognition (Latin, bundled) and ML Kit Document Scanner run on your device. Google Play services may download or refresh a model. Document images are not uploaded to a Paperstow server.

Data sharing

We do not sell or share your data. If you use Share or Archive, you choose the destination.

Children's privacy

This App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.
